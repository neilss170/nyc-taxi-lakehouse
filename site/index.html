What profiling found

  • Fare-total mismatch is a vendor convention, not corruption. The gap is almost always exactly $2.50 or $1.75: vendor 1 folds the congestion and airport fees into extra. So it is a warning, not an error.
  • A source gap, 21–24 Sep 2023. Those days hold under half the usual trips in the published file. Gold flags them and the forecast skips them.
  • Rule thresholds came from the data. Speed, duration, distance and fare limits were set after profiling the distributions, not guessed.
